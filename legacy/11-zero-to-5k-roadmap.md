# 11 — ZERO TO $5K ROADMAP

The bootstrap path. From zero capital to first $5,000 in revenue. Week-by-week.

This is the realistic version. No guru numbers. No "make $10K in your first month." Just what actually happens if you execute.

---

## 3. The launch phase (Weeks 4–8)

First posts go live. First sales come slow.

### Week 4 — First posts

Cadence kicks in: 14 main posts, 4 field, 5 notes per week.

**Realistic outcome:**
- Followers: 50–500 across the network (mostly main)
- Profile views: 1K–5K
- Store sessions: 100–500
- **Sales: 0–3 in week 1.**

This is normal. The brand is unknown. The algorithm is testing. The content is being categorized.

**Do not panic. Do not change strategy.**

### Week 5 — Hold the line

Continue posting cadence. Add weekly review (Sunday, 30 min).

```
[ ] Tag your top 3 hooks from week 4 (use again with variation)
[ ] Tag your bottom 3 — any pattern? (often: too long, hook too slow)
[ ] Generate next 30 mythologies for upcoming estates
[ ] Order more samples for next 2 estates ($100)
[ ] Inspect, photograph, build content library
```

**Realistic outcome:**
- Followers: 200–1,500 main account
- Sales: 1–8 in week 5
- **First estate may close (5 SKUs × 5 units = 25 units total)**

### Week 6 — First signal

Around now, one or two videos will start to outperform — 5K–50K views. This is normal "algorithm is finding your audience" behavior.

```
[ ] Identify the breakout video — what was the hook?
[ ] Recreate variations of that hook with different watches
[ ] Open second estate (next 5–7 SKUs)
[ ] First Klaviyo welcome flow conversions begin trickling in
```

**Realistic outcome:**
- Followers: 500–3,500 main account
- Sales: 3–15 in week 6
- **Cumulative revenue: $400–1,800**

### Week 7 — First viral attempt

If a video crosses 100K views in week 6 or 7, you have your first signal.

```
[ ] Don't celebrate. Don't change anything.
[ ] Continue cadence.
[ ] Restock any sold-out SKU
[ ] Generate the next 30 mythologies
[ ] Photograph next estate's SKUs
```

**Realistic outcome (if a video hits):**
- Followers: 2,000–15,000 main
- Sales: 8–40 in week 7
- **Cumulative revenue: $800–5,000**

If no video hits yet, that's also normal. Keep posting. The breakout typically happens between weeks 4–10.

### Week 8 — Compounding starts

```
[ ] Multiple SKUs across multiple estates active
[ ] Repeat purchases starting (Klaviyo post-purchase flow doing work)
[ ] First Ledger replies arriving (people responding to "correspondence is open")
[ ] First UGC unboxing videos appearing on TikTok
```

**Realistic outcome:**
- Followers: 3,000–25,000 main
- Sales: 15–70 in week 8
- **Cumulative revenue: $1,500–10,000**

**Most operators hit $5K cumulative revenue between weeks 6 and 10.** Some hit it in week 4 (rare). Some not until week 14 (also fine).

## 4. The week-by-week revenue progression (realistic)

Pessimistic / Median / Optimistic:

| Week | Pessimistic | Median | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | $0–200 | $200–500 | $500–1,500 |
| 5 | $200–500 | $500–1,200 | $1,500–4,000 |
| 6 | $500–1,200 | $1,200–2,500 | $4,000–10,000 |
| 7 | $1,200–2,500 | $2,500–5,000 | $10,000–25,000 |
| 8 | $2,500–4,000 | $5,000–10,000 | $25,000+ |
| **Cumulative through W8** | **$4,400–8,400** | **$9,400–19,200** | **$41,000+** |

**The $5K cumulative milestone:**
- Pessimistic: hit by end of Week 8–10
- Median: hit by end of Week 6–7
- Optimistic: hit by end of Week 4–5

If you're on the pessimistic track, **diagnose ruthlessly** before assuming it's just slow:

1. **Hook quality** — is your first 1.5 seconds boring? Re-read file 05 hook bank.
2. **Voice consistency** — are you breaking voice anywhere? (Audit captions, replies, emails)
3. **Photography** — does every photograph use the LUT? Are surfaces from approved list?
4. **Product fit** — does any SKU look "drop-shippy" on camera? Cut it.
5. **Posting consistency** — are you actually doing 14 main posts per week? Or skipping days?

The single most common failure: **inconsistent posting in weeks 1–4.** Most operators give up before the algorithm has categorized them.

## 5. The reinvestment ladder

As revenue comes in, where to redeploy:

### First $500 of revenue
- Reorder bestsellers (10–20 units each)
- Restock packaging materials

### First $1,000
- Above + upgrade ElevenLabs to Creator plan ($22/mo)
- Add Klaviyo paid plan if you cross 250 contacts
- Buy 6 months of domain + Shopify

### First $2,500
- Above + first bulk supplier order (50 units of top SKU)
- Order 200 dossier cards in bulk
- Begin building Reserve tier ($249–349 watches) — order samples

### First $5,000
- Above + LinkedIn / TikTok Spark ad budget ($300–500 for boosting top organic posts)
- Sourcing agent retainer if not already
- First Ledger physical correspondence (mail to top 20 customers)

**Do not** spend on:
- Influencer collaborations (breaks the world)
- Paid creative (use spark ads on organic only)
- A "real" photographer (the LUT is the look — pros over-polish it)
- Branded swag / stickers / tote bags (no merch yet)
- A logo redesign (the wordmark is fine)

## 8. The traps in the first 8 weeks

### Trap 1: Comparing yourself to TikTok success stories

Everyone you see on TikTok claiming "I made $30K in my first month dropshipping" is either lying or got astronomically lucky on one viral video. The median is what's described above. **Trust the median.**

### Trap 2: Pivoting in week 3

If sales are slow in weeks 4–5, you'll be tempted to "try something else." Don't. The algorithm needs 4–6 weeks minimum to categorize you. Pivoting resets the clock.

### Trap 3: Adding discounts in week 4

The first slow week, you'll be tempted to run a "20% off launch sale!" to kickstart sales. **Do not.** This is the single most destructive thing you can do to the brand.

If cash flow is tight, launch a smaller estate (3–4 items at higher prices) to your existing list.

### Trap 4: Posting less when nothing's working

The opposite is correct. If posts aren't landing, post MORE — but with iterations on the hook. Cadence is the algorithm signal.

### Trap 5: Trying to do paid ads in month 1

Don't. Your hooks aren't validated yet. You'll waste $300–500 on ads that don't convert. Wait until you have 3+ organic videos that crossed 50K views, then boost ONLY those.

### Trap 6: Showing your face once

The temptation: "If I just post one explanation video about who I am, people will trust the brand more." False. The mystery is the trust signal. The moment a face appears, the world becomes a guy with a Shopify store.

### Trap 7: Adding a popup

You'll see another ecom store with a "10% off if you sign up" popup converting at 5%, and you'll think "I should add that." Do not. Every conversion-optimization pattern works for ordinary ecom and breaks aesthetic ecom.

## 9. The mindset

The first 8 weeks feel slow because the compounding hasn't started. Imagine:
- You post 100 videos in weeks 1–4 — total views, maybe 50K.
- You post 100 videos in weeks 5–8 — total views, maybe 500K.
- You post 100 videos in weeks 9–12 — total views, maybe 5M.

That's how it works. **The first 100 videos are paying for the next 1,000.**

The operators who get to $5K are the ones who post the boring video #67 the same way they posted the first one. The discipline is the entire game.

## 10. The single most important rule for $0 → $5K

> **Post the schedule. Hold the voice. Don't pivot. Don't discount.**

Everything else is detail. If you do these four things for 8 weeks, you will hit $5K. If you don't, you won't — regardless of how good the brand bible is.

Read this every Monday morning before you start sourcing.
