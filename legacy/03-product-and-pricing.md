# 03 — PRODUCT & PRICING

What to source. What to reject. How to price. The three decisions that determine whether the brand is profitable.

---

## 1. The hero category

**Skeleton automatic, 38–40mm, off-white or charcoal dial, no logo.**

Why this wins:
- The exposed movement = hypnotic 4-second macro = TikTok retention
- 38–40mm reads as "considered" not "loud"
- No logo means no legal risk and full aesthetic control
- $12–20 sourcing, $129–179 sell price, ~75% margin after fees

Source: 1688 (primary), Yupoo (cross-reference for QC photos).

## 2. Catalog composition

Lock the catalog ratio. Your first three estates should look roughly like this:

| Category | % of SKUs | Price tier |
|---|---|---|
| Skeleton automatic (hero) | 35% | 129–179 |
| Bauhaus minimal automatic | 25% | 89–129 |
| Field/pilot-stripped | 15% | 89–129 |
| Square/cushion case | 10% | 129–179 |
| Pocket watches as desk objects | 5% | 89–129 |
| Brass/leather accessories | 10% | 49–89 |

**The accessories are critical.** They:
- Hit the $49–89 impulse layer
- Create cross-sell paths from watch buyers
- Photograph well in the world (loupes on documents, leather rolls on linen)
- Improve average order value via post-purchase upsell

## 3. Pricing tiers (locked)

| Tier | Price | % of catalog | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | `49` | 10% (accessories) | Door-opener, AOV builder |
| Soft entry | `89` | 20% | First-time buyer hook |
| Modal | `129` | 50% | The conversion sweet spot |
| Anchor | `179` | 15% | Hero pieces |
| Reserve | `249–349` | 4% | Margin layer, perceived-value anchor |
| Single Lot | `600–1200` | 1% | Pure margin, lore-driven (introduce month 4+) |

### Why $129 is the magic number

It sits between the impulse-trust threshold (~$80, where buyers don't trust quality) and the consideration threshold (~$200, where buyers research alternatives). For a 22–35yo man with disposable income, $129 is a "yes I'll just buy it" price.

### Pricing display rules

- **No `$` symbol.** Just the number, monospaced.
- **No compare-at prices.** No fake "was $299, now $129" anchoring.
- **No bundle discounts shown publicly.** Bundles only revealed in cart.
- **No tax-included pricing toggles.** Show base price, calculate tax in checkout.
- **Currency:** display in customer's local currency silently. Never show a switcher prominently.

### The discount policy

There are none. Forever.

- No Black Friday
- No Cyber Monday
- No newsletter signup discount
- No first-purchase discount
- No abandoned-cart discount
- No birthday emails
- No "we miss you" reactivation discount

The Ledger (early access) is the only privilege. That's it.

If a slow month hits and you're tempted: launch a smaller estate (3–4 items) instead. Narrative-justified scarcity, not discount-driven cash injection.

## 4. The reject list (hard)

If a product hits ANY of these, kill it. No exceptions.

- **Visually mimics a real luxury brand** (Rolex, AP, Patek, Cartier, Omega, etc.). Legal risk, takedown risk, brand risk. Source unbranded designs only.
- **Visible Chinese branding** that won't physically remove. Includes logos on dial, caseback engravings, or strap.
- **Smartwatch, hybrid, quartz with battery indicator visible.** Mechanical only. Quartz is acceptable only if it's silent and indistinguishable from automatic.
- **Plastic, resin, silicone case material.** Never.
- **Already viral on TikTok.** If you've seen it in three videos this month, it's saturated.
- **Dial larger than 42mm.** Reads as Invicta-shaped on camera.
- **Anything with a date complication that uses American date format** (e.g., visible "DEC 13"). Breaks European archival framing.
- **Anything that doesn't yield a hypnotic 4-second macro.** If you can't shoot one beautiful detail, it's not a hero piece.

## 5. The sample protocol

**Every SKU is physically inspected before listing. No exceptions.**

### Sample order (week 1)

- Pick 8–10 candidate hero watches
- Order from 2–3 different suppliers (not all from one)
- Budget: ~$200 for first sample batch
- Shipping: use the cheapest air shipping ($30–50). Express isn't needed at sample stage.

### Inspection checklist (run for every sample)

```
[ ] Dial finish: any defects, dust under crystal, misaligned indices
[ ] Crown action: smooth, audible click, no looseness
[ ] Movement: winds smoothly, ticks consistent, runs >24h after winding
[ ] Caseback: removable for service, no obvious counterfeit markings
[ ] Lug width: matches advertised (for strap interchangeability)
[ ] Strap quality: stitching tight, leather genuine or convincingly aged
[ ] Weight: feels substantial, not toy-like
[ ] Scratches/damage in transit: any unacceptable defects
[ ] Photographs well: shoot a 4-second macro before deciding
[ ] Sound: ticking should be barely audible, satisfying
```

### The kill criteria

Reject the supplier (not just the unit) if:
- More than 15% of samples have visible defects
- Dial misalignment is consistent across samples
- Crown click is inconsistent
- Movement runs <18 hours
- Communication is poor (slow replies, broken English template responses)

**The cost of a bad supplier is one viral "this is just AliExpress" video. Always order samples first.**

## 6. Sourcing workflow

### Primary: 1688

- Use a sourcing agent if you don't speak Chinese: Yansourcing, BasetaoSourcing, or Sourcinglion. They charge $5–10/order in service fees and handle communication.
- Pay via Alipay through your agent. Never pay direct unless you've worked with the supplier 3+ times.
- Order quantity: 5–10 units per SKU for first batch. Scale to 20–50 once a SKU proves out.
- Lead time: 7–14 days production + 5–8 days air shipping = ~3 weeks total.

### Secondary: Yupoo

- Yupoo sellers are typically Guangzhou-based wholesalers with photo-heavy catalogs.
- Use Yupoo to discover new watch styles, then back-trace the model on 1688 for better pricing.
- Direct purchase via Yupoo: contact via WhatsApp, pay via Western Union or Wise. Higher risk, higher prices, faster shipping.

### Tertiary: AliExpress (only for testing)

- Don't build a brand on AliExpress. Margins too thin, lead times too long, quality variable.
- Use only to test a new SKU before committing to a 1688 bulk order.

## 7. Inventory model

### Phase 1: Pre-launch (months 0–1)

- Hold ~20 SKUs across 3 estates
- 5–10 units per SKU
- ~$400–600 total inventory cost
- Stored at home

### Phase 2: Validation (months 2–3)

- 30–40 active SKUs
- 10–20 units of proven SKUs, 5 units of new
- ~$1500–2500 inventory cost
- Still home-stored or 3PL trial

### Phase 3: Scale (months 4–6)

- 60+ SKUs
- 30–50 units of bestsellers
- ~$8K–15K inventory cost
- Move to 3PL (ShipBob, ShipMonk, or direct from supplier with branded packaging insert sent to fulfillment center)

### Phase 4: Compound (months 7–12)

- 100+ SKUs
- Bulk orders predicted by sell-through rate
- ~$30K–60K inventory cost
- Full 3PL with packaging assembled in-fulfillment

## 9. The product-page metadata block

Every product page has this exact metadata block. Keep formatting identical across every SKU.

```
LOT        0049-A
ORIGIN     Geneva, est. 1990s
CONDITION  Excellent, minor wear
MOVEMENT   Mechanical automatic
CASE       Stainless, 38mm
CRYSTAL    Mineral, lightly scratched
STRAP      Leather, brown, aged
RESERVE    ~38 hours
WEIGHT     ~62g
LOT SIZE   1 of 7
```

Notes:
- `LOT SIZE` shows real inventory (e.g., "1 of 7"). When sold to "1 of 0," mark item `archived` rather than removing.
- `ORIGIN` is fictional but should match the estate's location.
- `CONDITION` always "Excellent" or "Excellent, minor wear." Never "New" — the world doesn't sell new objects.
- Be honest about CRYSTAL/MOVEMENT specs. If it's mineral crystal, say mineral. If it's a Seagull movement, say "Mechanical automatic" (which it is). Don't lie about specs.
