# 07 — MYTHOLOGY BANK

50 pre-written mythology templates ready to plug into product pages, captions, and voiceovers. Replace bracketed placeholders with your specific lot details.

These are seeds. Generate variations using the Claude prompt in [06-ai-workflow.md](./06-ai-workflow.md). Never use one verbatim across multiple products — vary the rhythm.

## How to use this bank

### For product pages

Pick one mythology from 1–40. Use as the product description, exactly. Match the lot's actual fictional origin (the city in the mythology should match the estate it belongs to).

### For TikTok captions

Use a closing line (41–50) plus a fragment of the main mythology. Example:

```
LOT 0049-A

Recovered from an estate in Trieste.
The previous owner left only a name 
written on the inside of the case.

available now
```

### For voiceovers

Generate the audio for each mythology using ElevenLabs, save as `.wav`, name by mythology number. Reuse across multiple watches by changing only the lot number.

### For Klaviyo emails

Use a mythology as the body of an estate-launch email. Frame it as an internal handler memo:

> *Subject: Estate 049 opens Tuesday at 19:00 GMT.*
>
> *Recovered from a closed brokerage in Antwerp. Found in a desk drawer alongside three unsent letters. Eleven items have been catalogued. The estate opens to Ledger members tomorrow at 19:00 GMT.*
>
> *— Section B*

## The variation rule

If you've used a mythology in the last 30 days, generate a variation rather than reusing. Claude prompt:

> Take this mythology and generate 5 variations. Same voice, same length, same structure. Vary the city, year, and provenance detail.

That generates 250 unique mythologies from this bank of 50. Enough for a year of content.

---

## The connection rule (advanced)

Once you have 20+ products live, start cross-referencing estates. A mythology in Estate 067 mentions "a man who left Milan in 2003" — and Estate 049 (sealed 2003 in Milan) is the implied source. Hardcore fans will find this. They'll screenshot it. That's the moment your brand becomes a cult.

Track all fictional names, dates, and locations in a single Notion table called `World Bible`. Reference it before generating any new mythology to maintain internal consistency.
