# 12 — TWELVE-MONTH ROADMAP

The path from $5K cumulative → $150K/month. Realistic milestones, what to do at each, and what to avoid.

---

## Month 1 — Foundation & launch

**Goal:** validate the system can produce content, ship orders, and convert.

**Targets:**
- 0–20 orders cumulative
- 1,000–5,000 main account followers
- 250+ Klaviyo subscribers
- Cumulative revenue: $0–$3,000

**Operator focus:**
- Post the cadence relentlessly
- Inspect every sample
- Don't announce, don't push, don't discount
- Read this kit every Monday

**Spend:** ~$400 (initial inventory + tools + packaging)

**Don't yet:**
- Run paid ads
- Hire VAs
- Launch Ledger
- Add Reserve tier

---

## Month 2 — First signal

**Goal:** identify what's working. Double down on top hooks.

**Targets:**
- 30–80 orders this month
- 5,000–25,000 main account followers
- 1,000+ Klaviyo subscribers
- Monthly revenue: $3,000–$10,000

**Operator focus:**
- Re-shoot variations of top-performing hooks
- Source 2–3 new estates worth of inventory
- Build out @brand.field with consistent ambient cadence
- First Ledger replies arrive (people responding to "correspondence is open")

**Reinvest:**
- Bulk reorder bestsellers (50 units each)
- Order 200 dossier cards in bulk
- Upgrade ElevenLabs Creator ($22/mo)
- Begin Reserve tier sample sourcing

**Common breakout:** one Detail post crosses 500K views around week 6–8. The follower count jumps. **Hold the line. Don't change anything in response.**

---

## Month 3 — Inflection or diagnosis

**Goal:** confirm the model works. Or diagnose ruthlessly.

**Targets:**
- 100–300 orders this month
- 15,000–60,000 main account followers
- 3,000+ Klaviyo subscribers
- Monthly revenue: $10,000–$35,000

**If hitting targets:**
- Launch The Ledger formally
- Open Reserve tier ($249–$349) with 2–3 SKUs
- First Klaviyo quarterly correspondence newsletter
- Begin spark ads — boost only top 3 organic videos at $20–50/day

**If missing targets, diagnose:**

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low views | Hooks too slow | Re-read hook bank, shorten hooks to <1.5s |
| Views but no clicks | Bio link not compelling | Test "→ archive" or just URL |
| Clicks but no orders | Product page weak | Audit metadata block, mythology, photography |
| Orders but no repeat | Post-purchase flow weak | Re-read file 09 |
| Repeats but stagnant total | Inventory too narrow | Open more estates faster |

---

## Month 4 — Scale operations

**Goal:** move from operator-doing-everything to operator-managing-systems.

**Targets:**
- 300–800 orders this month
- 30,000–100,000 main account followers
- 6,000+ Klaviyo subscribers
- 50–150 Ledger members
- Monthly revenue: $30,000–$100,000

**Operator focus:**
- Hire first VA for fulfillment + comment management ($400–800/mo)
- VA training: 1 week shadow + voice study (file 10, section 9)
- Move inventory to 3PL (ShipBob, ShipMonk) once daily orders > 30
- 3PL transition: pre-assemble inserts, audit aggressively
- First Single Lot ($600–$1,200) — test the high-margin tier

**Don't yet:**
- Hire a second VA
- Move into apparel
- Spin off second world

**Cash flow:** revenue should now be $1K+/day net. Reinvest 60%, hold 40% as buffer.

---

## Month 5 — Compounding begins

**Goal:** lock in repeat-buyer flywheel.

**Targets:**
- 500–1,200 orders this month
- 60,000–150,000 main account followers
- Repeat purchase rate: 25%+
- Monthly revenue: $60,000–$150,000

**Operator focus:**
- First @brand.field UGC repost stream (customer unboxings as "field reports")
- Quarterly correspondence newsletter to all customers (long-form, world-building)
- Single Lots become regular cadence (1 per estate)
- Spark ads: $100–300/day on top 5 organic videos

**Watch out for:**
- 3PL quality slipping (audit by ordering from your own store)
- VA voice drift (weekly review)
- Inventory bottlenecks (predict bulk orders 4 weeks out)

---

## Month 6 — The cult forms

**Goal:** cross the threshold from "ecom store" to "brand customers identify with."

**Targets:**
- 800–2,000 orders this month
- 100,000–250,000 main account followers
- 200+ Ledger members
- Monthly revenue: $100,000–$200,000

**Operator focus:**
- First "guest estate" collaboration with another faceless aesthetic creator (no influencers — niche peers only)
- Apparel-as-objects test: ONE item that "belongs to the world" (heavy plain wool scarf, leather card sleeve, monogrammed cotton handkerchief). Never branded apparel.
- Year-end Volume publication planning (file 13)
- Hire second VA: customer correspondence + content scheduling

**Brand health checks:**
- Re-read brand bible (file 01)
- Audit all touchpoints for voice violations
- Check that 6 months of consistent visual output is in place

---

## Month 7–9 — Scale the world

**Goal:** durable monthly revenue. Begin building the moat in earnest.

**Targets:**
- 1,500–3,500 orders/month
- 200,000–500,000 main account followers
- 500+ Ledger members
- Monthly revenue: $150,000–$350,000

**Strategic moves:**

**1. The "Volume" publication.** A small printed book or zine collecting the year's mythologies. ~80 pages, $30–$50, 200–500 unit print run. Profit immaterial, brand equity infinite. Send free to top 20 Ledger members.

**2. Sub-world test.** A second TikTok account exploring an adjacent fiction (e.g., a cartographer's archive, an audio engineer's estate). Same operator, same voice, different angle. Tests whether the system is brand-specific or operator-specific.

**3. Higher-tier Reserve.** Pieces in the $400–$600 range with custom aging done in-house (force-patina'd dials, hand-distressed straps). 30–50% margin maintained.

**4. Spark ad scale.** $300–$1,000/day on top organic. Always boost organic, never run dedicated creative.

---

## Month 10 — The moat is real

**Goal:** establish defensibility competitors can't compress.

**Targets:**
- 2,500–5,000 orders/month
- 300,000–700,000 main account followers
- 1,000+ Ledger members
- Monthly revenue: $250,000–$500,000

**Strategic moves:**

**1. The Volume drops.** Ledger members get first pick. Sells out in 48 hours.

**2. First "Single Lot" series.** A set of 5–10 truly unique objects, each $1,000–$3,000. Sourced from real estate sales (or carefully customized from quality vintage). Tests whether the audience will pay real-collector prices to a faceless brand. They will.

**3. Audit the visual archive.** 12 months of consistent posts, all using the same LUT, voice, and grammar. This is the moat. New entrants face a year-long cold start.

---

## Month 11 — Defend & expand

**Goal:** lock in the position before copycats arrive (they will).

**Targets:**
- 3,000–6,000 orders/month
- Monthly revenue: $300,000–$600,000

**Strategic moves:**

**1. Cross-pollinate the sub-world.** If the sub-world test in months 7–9 worked, scale it: separate Shopify, separate Klaviyo, but operationally shared.

**2. Operator hire.** Bring on a content lead — someone who can run the cadence in your voice without you producing every reel. **Critical hire. Voice violation = brand damage.**

**3. Real-world signal.** Place objects in 2–3 carefully chosen physical locations (a specific bookstore, a specific hotel, a specific gallery). Not for revenue — for cultural proof.

---

## Month 12 — The compounding moat

**Goal:** durable $300K+/month with defensible position.

**Targets:**
- 4,000–8,000 orders/month
- 400,000–1,000,000 main account followers
- 1,500+ Ledger members
- Monthly revenue: $400,000–$800,000+

**Year-end review:**

```
[ ] Total revenue: $X

[ ] Repeat rate: X%
[ ] Ledger size: X

[ ] Visual continuity preserved? (audit)
[ ] Voice continuity preserved? (audit)
[ ] Discounts run: 0 (this is the discipline)
[ ] Faces shown: 0 (this is the moat)
```

## Year 2 outlook

If year 1 ends at $5M–$8M ARR:

**Possible paths:**

1. **Hold and compound.** Run the same system for year 2 with optimization. Expect 50–100% growth via repeat buyers + Ledger compounding. Year 2 revenue: $8M–$15M.

2. **Spin off sub-worlds.** Build 2–3 adjacent brands using the same operator playbook. Each at $1M–$3M ARR. Total $10M–$25M.

3. **License the aesthetic.** Major fashion brands or hotels license your aesthetic universe for capsule collaborations. Capital-light, brand-equity-heavy.

4. **Sell.** A faceless brand at $5M+ ARR with documented systems is acquirable for 3–5x multiple by aggregators (Thrasio-style, but the niche aesthetic version). $15M–$40M exit.

Most operators take path 1 or 2. Path 3 is rarer (requires 18+ months of cultural credibility). Path 4 is fastest cash but lowest cultural payoff.

## What kills this in the next 12 months

Failure modes ranked by frequency:

1. **Voice drift around month 3.** Operator gets bored, "experiments" with new tone. Brand collapses within 60 days.

2. **Discount panic in month 4.** First slow month after viral peak triggers a "Black Friday sale!" Brand never recovers credibility.

3. **VA hire without voice training.** First VA replies in casual tone. 30 reviews mention "weird customer service." Brand health drops.

4. **3PL quality lapse.** Operator stops auditing unboxings. Customers receive boxes without dossier cards. UGC dries up. Repeat rate drops.

5. **Showing face for "credibility."** Operator does one "story time" video. Brand mythology breaks. Ledger members unsubscribe.

6. **Trying to "leverage" TikTok virality with paid ads on dedicated creative.** Ads convert poorly because they don't fit the world. $5K wasted, brand voice diluted in the algorithm.

Avoid all six of these. The discipline is the moat.
